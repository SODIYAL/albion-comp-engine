# data/ — the evidence layer

Versioned source-of-truth for everything the engine knows that is NOT the
game's own data. Four concepts, kept strictly apart — raw popularity,
killboard sightings, or community submissions never automatically become
"ideal" recommendations:

| concept | where | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| game facts | `source_pins.yaml` + `pipeline/out/` | items/spells/ranges from ONE pinned ao-bin-dumps commit, manifest-verified |
| published builds | `published_comps/`, `published_builds/`, `armory_imports/` | what named sources actually published, verbatim + provenance |
| loadout observations | `pipeline/out/weapon_usage_v2.json`, `pipeline/out/party_rosters.json.gz` (killer parties with full gear, nightly harvest), companion imports | what players were SEEN wearing — prevalence and kit doctrine evidence, never advice |
| canonical builds | `canonical_builds/` + generated flags in `builds_index.json` | human-reviewed defaults that cleared the promotion gate below |

Generated views: `pipeline/build_builds.py` normalizes everything here into
`pipeline/out/builds_index.json` (ordered by the selection order below,
canonical defaults flagged with their basis) and writes the
`validation_result` record to `pipeline/out/builds_validation.json`.
Human-maintained files stay VERBATIM; every derived value is generated and
carries the basis it rests on; unknown stays unknown. The gates:
`tests/test_builds.py` (H5–H21) and `tests/test_provenance.py`.

## Provenance

- `source_pins.yaml` pins ONE `ao-bin-dumps` commit (`repository`, `commit`,
  `commit_timestamp`, `environment`, the nearest `game_patch`, the file
  list). Every game-data input is fetched from that exact commit by
  `pipeline/fetch_snapshot.py`; nothing follows a moving branch. The
  manifest (`pipeline/out/source_manifest.json`) records what was actually
  fetched with a SHA-256 per file; any drift between the pin, the manifest
  and the derived outputs blocks the release (`build_dataset.py` exit 2).
- Every published record names the snapshot it was normalized against
  (`snapshot_commit`) and the game `patch` it belongs to. Adapter batches
  are re-validated for equippability against the pinned snapshot on every
  build, so a stale import fails closed instead of shipping; `stale` is the
  status for a record whose patch is behind the current pin.
- Tier, enchant, quality and item power are separate fields, unknown unless
  the source stated them; zero-to-nonzero tier transitions and nested
  enchantment item power are preserved per tier and level, never collapsed.
- Spell and gear references resolve to stable UniqueNames from the pinned
  snapshot (the item bank keeps every tier's raw id). A numeric pick such
  as `q3` resolves to the exact pool entry (1-based, game order); an index
  outside the pool is quarantined as unknown, never rounded to a neighbour.

## Record conventions

Every document carries `kind`, `id`, `source`, `content`, `party_size`,
`approval` and (for comps) `parties`. Third-party text is data: slot rows,
skill indices, gear names and notes stay VERBATIM in `*_raw` fields, and
normalization to exact UniqueNames is GENERATED by `pipeline/build_builds.py`
(`pipeline/out/builds_index.json`), never hand-written into the record.

- `source` — `kind` (one of the source kinds below), `family` (the
  author/site: `caller:<name>`, `metabattle`, `armory`, `companion:local`),
  `author`, `url`, `record` (what was read, where), `license` (MetaBattle
  content is CC BY-SA and the attribution travels on every record).
- `published`, `ingested`, `observed`, `patch`, `snapshot_commit` — dates
  and pins are data and keep their values.
- `approval` — `status` (below), `basis` (what the status rests on; an
  explicit shotcaller approval says so in the basis and names the approver
  in `approved_by`) and `as_of`.
- Alternatives are structured, never prose: a source's "Dawns/Rotcaller"
  becomes `weapon_alternatives`, "Aegis/Taproot" becomes
  `gear_alternatives`.
- Unknowns are stored explicitly (`None` plus an `unknowns` list), never
  omitted and never inferred. Armor/head/shoes actives exist in the schema
  and are unknown where a caller sheet does not name them. Nothing ever
  silently resolves to option 1.
- A record's `confidence` is scored per dimension from what the source
  actually stated: `item_mapping`, `spell_mapping`, `patch`,
  `content_context`, `party_size`, `loadout_completeness`; two dimensions
  are stored as explicit nulls — `source_independence` (judged across
  records, not per record) and `outcome` (no outcome evidence exists).

### Source kinds

`caller_sheet`, `metabattle`, `armory_manual`, `companion`, `killboard`,
`manual_link`, `murderledger`, `solo_1v1` (`SOURCE_KINDS` in
`pipeline/builds_lib.py`). `murderledger` and `solo_1v1` are 1v1/duel
sources: validation rejects any such record whose party-size range reaches
past 2, and selection never offers them for larger requests. A record with
an unknown source kind, an unknown status or a missing family is rejected.

### Statuses and quarantine

`raw → normalized → (quarantined | candidate) → approved`, plus `stale` and
`rejected` (`STATUSES` in `pipeline/builds_lib.py`). Imports are never born
approved: adapter output begins as `candidate` (or `quarantined`). A record
whose normalization is not clean — an invalid spell index, a reference the
pinned snapshot cannot resolve — is `quarantined`: it stays in the index as
evidence that needs fixing, its `quarantined_fields` are listed in
`builds_validation.json`, and it carries no promotion weight and is never a
default. Files with `example: true` are format documentation and are never
ingested.

### Promotion gate and selection order

A canonical default requires ONE of (`canonical_eligible` in
`pipeline/builds_lib.py`):

- sanctioned/current official Armory evidence + independent validation;
- agreement across two or more genuinely independent source FAMILIES —
  records sharing a family (same author or site) count once;
- explicit current shotcaller approval (a shotcaller-authored sheet carries
  this for the author's own comp).

Quarantined and rejected records carry no promotion weight; a single-family
candidate group cannot become canonical. Displayed-build ordering for one
(weapon, content) group (`selection_order`): clean records before
quarantined/rejected ones, then approval rank (approved, candidate,
normalized, raw, stale, quarantined, rejected), then patch freshness (newer
first), then confidence/source agreement, then stable id — never arrival
order. A comp or build tagged with a broader content than one template is
offered under the templates it covers and ALWAYS marked as a fallback.
Manual pins (`canonical_builds/`) choose between eligible records; a pin
cannot promote a record whose evidence does not qualify.

## Source policy

- **MetaBattle** — automated via their MediaWiki Action API
  (`pipeline/adapters/metabattle.py`), CC BY-SA attribution on every record.
  Adapter v2 captures every group-PvP category (ZvZ, Hellgate 5v5/10v10,
  Crystal League/Arena, Ganking; solo/PvE modes stay out) into
  `published_builds/metabattle.yaml`, each record's `content` derived from
  the page's own mode category. Fixtures carry page id, revision id and
  revision timestamp so the parser is testable offline.
- **Official Armory** — manual imports only (`armory_imports/README.md`);
  no reverse-engineering of game traffic or private endpoints.
- **Albion Online Grind** (<https://albiononlinegrind.com/group-builds>) and
  **Albion Free Market** (<https://albionfreemarket.com/builds>) — NOT
  scraped or bulk-ingested without permission. Manual imports with a source
  link are welcome as `kind: manual_link` records; a sanctioned adapter can
  slot in beside the MetaBattle one later.
- **Albion Online Data Project** (<https://www.albion-online-data.com/api/>)
  — optional build-PRICE enrichment only. Price is never build quality.
- **MurderLedger and other 1v1 sources** — restricted to solo/1v1 contexts;
  the schema validator rejects any such record whose party-size range
  reaches past 2, and they have zero eligibility for 10-20-player
  recommendations.
- **Usage samples** declare their fight-size semantics (fight size, not party
  or side size); per-battle records keep party and side size explicitly
  unknown, abilities are stored as unknown (the killboard never exposes
  them), and battle-level aggregation exists beside correlated player counts.

Nothing in this directory feeds Forge scoring. Displayed equipment/spell
loadouts may come from canonical builds. Gear itself IS scored — through the
curated gear sheets in `pipeline/sheets/gear/` and `build_extra`, never
through anything recorded here. Composition exclusions (a weapon barred from
a size band) each carry an evidence record, and no excluded weapon carries an
approved or canonical record at that size.
