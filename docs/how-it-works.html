03The question

“What gets better if this weapon joins now?”

Every allowed weapon faces the same test against the party as it stands. Empty jobs matter a lot. Jobs that are already full matter much less.

++

Save a must-have job

If a team has no real healing or no one who can stand up front, fixing that hole gets a huge lift.

+

Fill an empty job

A useful tool is worth most when the party does not have it yet.

↔

Help two players click

Some tools work better together, like pulling enemies into a big area hit.

·

Use a small trusted nudge

A hand-reviewed “solid pick” note can separate close choices. It never replaces the main test.

−

Avoid waste

Too much of a full job, an extra copy, or a trick that only counts once can push a choice down.

?
This is why there is no forever-best weapon.

Hallowfall can be a giant help to a party with no healer and a smaller help to a party whose healing is already covered. The team changes the answer.

05Honest boundaries

Some things are shown, but do not get a vote.

Comp Forge keeps the team-building engine separate from interesting evidence. That stops “popular” from quietly becoming “best.”

Killboard popularity

The planner can show which weapons were seen in similar-sized fights. That is useful context, not proof that a weapon wins.

Does it choose the forged team? No.

Example builds and teams

Real and hand-reviewed examples help explain how people equip and use weapons. They stay visible as examples.

Do examples secretly rerank picks? No.

Armor, food, and the rest

The planner can suggest a kit after the weapon is chosen. Today, full-team Forge chooses weapons and their move sets—not the whole gear bag.

Used while forging the weapon team? Not yet.

One small exception is visible: hand-reviewed “trusted pick” notes can give a tiny nudge when choices are close. Live popularity data still never votes.
