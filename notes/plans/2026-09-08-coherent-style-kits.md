# Coherent Builds + Style-Conditioned Kit Doctrine — implementation plan (2026-09-08)

Status: implemented 2026-09-08 (R29–R33, R24b; VALIDATION.md index 09-08
"Coherent builds and style cells"). The task checkboxes are the plan as
written; every task below is done.

**Goal:** Let the archetype chain carry a coherent build past the chest, and give every seat style-conditioned kit cells that fire on a DECLARED style only.

**Architecture:** The doctrine miner in `pipeline/build_dataset.py` keeps its shape; its chain guard is rescaled from counts to shares, and the miner gains a `style` parameter that filters the killboard builds to parties of one style (linked through a new tiny `pipeline/party_link.py`) and writes into `kit_styles.<style>` on the seat record. A new explicit step `pipeline/derive_party_styles.py` labels parties from the COMMITTED artifact with the engine's weapons-only identity. Both engine ports read the cell through the ONE doctrine reader `_seat_kit`, merged over the band.

**Tech stack:** Python 3 (`py -3`), script-style tests (`py -3 tests/<file>.py`, exit 0 = pass, NOT pytest), the JS port in `engine/app_scoring.js` (binary to grep — use `Select-String` or an editor), `dashboard/build.py` bundler.

**Spec:** `notes/specs/2026-09-08-coherent-style-kits-design.md`

## Global constraints

### Task 1: Rescale the chain guard — done (R29)

- Files: `pipeline/build_dataset.py:1170-1245` (`_modal_build_chain`); test `tests/test_roles.py` (`t_chain_guard`).
- Interfaces: `_modal_build_chain(build_dicts, uni, effect_map, gear, normalize)` — `build_dicts` = list of `(gear_dict, weight, player)`; gear_dict keys are raw slot names (`"Armor"`, `"Head"`, ...); returns `{slot: [id, n, of]}`. Same signature and return shape after; two new module constants `CHAIN_POCKET_SHARE = 0.20`, `CHAIN_POCKET_VOTES = 20` (module level, next to `KIT_SLOT_MAP`).
- Test R29 (synthetic populations): (A) a 30% chest pocket whose wearers all share helmet + shoes, against 70 builds spread over three other chests — the old guard (30 < 0.5 × 70) stopped at the chest; shares pass and the chain carries the helmet and shoes. (B) the 2026-09-04 Greataxe shape — the chain narrows to an 8-build pocket by the shoes step (8 of 74 = 11% < 20%, < 20 votes): the 7-of-8 cape inside it is never picked. (C) a pick under half the unconditional modal's SHARE stops: helmet A is 8 of the 30-build pocket (27%) while helmet X is 70% of everyone (half = 0.35). Population C must make CHEST_A the modal chest (the 70 others split `CHEST_B` × 25, `CHEST_C` × 25, `CHEST_D` × 20, all wearing `HELM_X`), or the chain picks CHEST_B and its 70/70 helmet would not stop.
- Implementation: `uncond_share[slot] = max(count) / total_w`; per step the pocket must hold `CHAIN_POCKET_SHARE * total_w` OR `CHAIN_POCKET_VOTES` votes, the pick needs >= 2 distinct players, >= `CHAIN_MIN_SHARE` of the pocket, and `n / pool_w >= 0.5 * uncond_share[slot]` — shares against shares (the count-against-count form failed by construction once the pocket was under half the population, stopping 69 of 118 weapons after one or two slots). Docstring updated: the pocket floor and the share-against-share guard are the 2026-09-08 rescale; measured on the 2,042-battle harvest they lift weapons reaching 4+ slots from 6 to 37.
- Steps: R29 fails with `a={'armor': ['CHEST_A', 30, 100]}` → implement → `29/29 role-layer tests passed` → rebuild (`release_clean: true`) → measure the depth distribution over `kit_weapon_build` for the VALIDATION entry (expected: the 4+ bucket well above 6; the miner's own uniform gating may differ from the probe's 37 by a few) → kit-affected gates: roles, golden, forge, validation_modes (a golden or forge pin moving because a kit chest changed is a STOP for a maintainer decision — the guard is never retuned to satisfy a pin) → commit `Chain guard rescaled: shares against shares, pocket floor (spec 2026-09-08)`.
