# R3: win-lift per weapon, pair and copy count on killer parties of 10-20

## Context

This is a report-only measurement for the outcome layer (BACKLOG "The outcome layer, after the
party-level read"). The question: does a weapon, a pair or a copy count go with winning beyond the
numbers each side brought? Association, not cause. Nothing here is a scoring input, and no template
or prior changes.

- **Artifact.** The committed killer-party artifact at origin/main 6590a51,
  `pipeline/out/party_rosters.json.gz`, sha256
  `829c8c293b0735393e66cad37602562a8d6e162c9bfc8ab77992b7c23270ba4b` (the hash
  `pipeline/out/party_styles.json` records): 362,212 battles, 447,130 parties, 1,748,183 builds.
- **Battle rosters.** The numbers control and the member-level guilds come from a read-only snapshot of
  the harvest cache (a read-only snapshot, 37,626 battle-list
  battles, last write 2026-10-08T19:54:12Z), opened read-only and decoded with `party_store.decode`.
- **Population, labels, numbers and split** are exactly what `pipeline/audit_capability_outcomes.py`
  applies; its `eligible`, `label`, `side_numbers` and `numbers_row` are imported, not copied.
  - Population: the battle-list population; parties of 10-20 with every weapon known and in the
    catalogue, and at least half the members in the fight.
  - Labels: win is kills >= 2 x deaths, loss is kills < deaths, and the traded-even middle is left
    out.
  - Numbers: log(own side / largest other alliance) and log(battle roster size) from the battle's
    roster, beside party size.
  - Split: training battle % 5 != 0, holdout battle % 5 == 0.
  - The counts reproduce the audit's: 16,638 labelled training parties (12,424 wins) over 9,199
    battles and 4,211 holdout parties (3,215 wins) over 2,411 battles. 2,278 parties traded even,
    2,167 were ineligible and 6,490 sit in the kill-feed poll population. Every labelled party has its
    side's numbers.
- **Item power.** The party record carries no item-power field. Each build carries the player's
  `AverageItemPower` (killer, victim and participant sightings; `GroupMembers` report 0).
  - Linkage: builds are linked to their party through `party_link.link_build`, by the `party` index
    for 248,434 builds and by the unique (battle, weapon) fallback for 37,131.
  - Party value: the mean over the party's linked builds when it has at least 3.
  - Coverage: 16,061 training parties (96.5%) and 4,076 holdout parties; mean 1,462, sd 123.
- **Primary guild.** The guild with most members in the party. It is read from the cache's party
  record after the analyzer's own member-overlap dedupe, re-run exactly. A cache party is matched to
  the artifact party on size and sorted weapons, which holds for 31,755 of the 31,784 parties of 10+.
  The training split has 926 primary guilds and the holdout 592.

## Method

**The control ladder.** Each effect is fitted four times. x is the effect's indicator; "numbers" is
the audit's control (side ratio, roster size, party size).

| Level | Model | Unit | Standard error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | logit: win ~ numbers + x | log-odds | cluster-robust over battles |
| L1 | logit: win ~ numbers + item power + x | log-odds | cluster-robust over battles |
| L2 | linear probability: win ~ numbers + item power + x, pooled | win-rate points (pp) | cluster-robust over primary guilds |
| L3 | as L2, with primary-guild fixed effects (within-guild) | win-rate points (pp) | cluster-robust over primary guilds |

- **The item-power control** is nine decile dummies on training cut points (1,288 / 1,366 / 1,417 /
  1,460 / 1,494 / 1,519 / 1,541 / 1,562 / 1,590). A single linear term over-corrects:
  - the item-power effect is steep in the top two deciles;
  - with the linear term, a weapon's lift correlates -0.39 across weapons with how much richer its
    parties are (the decile control: +0.06).
  - The linear-control run is kept beside the main one as a robustness read.
- L1 to L3 are fitted on the parties with item power and a primary guild known. L3 is identified only
  from guilds whose parties field the effect both ways; the tables give that count.
- **Effects.**
  - Weapon: x = the weapon fielded.
  - Pair: x = both fielded, with each member's own indicator in the model, so the pair term is the
    lift beyond both members' presence.
  - Copies: the levels 1 / 2 / 3+ copies against none (L0), and the contrasts "2 vs 1" and "3+ vs 1"
    on the parties fielding 1 or k copies (the lift of extra copies). 3+ is split out where it has 100
    training parties; otherwise the levels are 1 / 2+.
- **Floors.**
  - Weapon: 200 training parties fielding it and 200 not (98 of 136 fielded weapons pass).
  - Holdout check: 40 holdout parties fielding the weapon.
  - Pair: 200 training parties fielding both, and 100 fielding each member without the other (1,200
    of the 4,747 co-fielded pairs among the 98 weapons pass).
  - Copies: 100 training parties at the level (37 weapons, 50 contrasts).
  - Size bands: 150 parties fielding the weapon inside the band.
- **Intervals and tests.** 90% intervals come from a cluster bootstrap over battles (200 resamples,
  percentile), for every weapon at L0 and L1, every copy level and the reported pairs. p-values are
  two-sided Wald tests on the robust SE. Benjamini-Hochberg at 10% runs within each family (weapons,
  pairs, copy contrasts) at each level.
- **Holdout.** The same model is refitted on the holdout. A survivor "reproduces" when the holdout
  estimate has the training sign; the stricter count is a one-sided p < 0.05 in the training
  direction.
- **Reliability across effects.** This compares the correlation of the training and holdout estimates
  with the correlation that noise alone allows when the training spread is real. The true spread is
  estimated as tau^2 = var(estimates) - mean(SE^2), and the expected r is
  tau^2 / sqrt((tau^2 + SE_train^2)(tau^2 + SE_hold^2)).
- **Guild-disjoint replication.** The battle-split holdout shares guilds with the training split, so
  even a within-guild lift can reproduce on it through the same guild fielding the same weapon. The
  primary guilds are therefore also split in two halves (crc32 of the name). Each half's per-weapon
  lifts are fitted at every level over all battles, and the halves are compared.
- **Size bands.** The weapon ladder is rerun inside 10-14 and 15-20.

## The numbers

### Baseline: what numbers and gear do

On the training split the numbers model reads:

- log side ratio: +1.09 per log unit (se 0.04);
- log roster size: -0.35 (se 0.03);
- party size: +0.024 (se 0.006).

Item power adds a large, separate term. Against the lowest decile (1,288 or less), the decile
coefficients climb +0.43 / +0.45 / +0.52 / +0.84 / +0.70 / +0.90 / +0.96 / +1.46 / +1.92. The top
decile (1,590 and above) is worth +1.92 log-odds over the lowest; doubling the side ratio is worth
about +0.8. A linear term reads +0.48 per sd (123 item power). Gear is the first confound of any
weapon lift.

- **Concentration.** Among the pairs shown, the most concentrated are Enigmatic Staff + Witchwork
  Staff (the most frequent guild holds 25% of the parties) and the Wailing Bow pairs (19-22%).

## Verdict

**Per-weapon win-lift does not carry a reliable weapon signal at this corpus size.** It carries a
reliable signal of who fields a weapon.

- **The numbers-only lift is real and replicates.** 39 of 98 weapons survive BH at 10%, all 39 keep
  their sign on battles the fit never saw, and the training and holdout lifts correlate at r = 0.81.
- **It is mostly gear.** It tracks how much richer the fielding parties are (r = 0.67 across weapons),
  and the ten most negative lifts all belong to cheap-gear weapons.
- **It is mostly guild.** Primary-guild fixed effects leave 6 of 98 weapons, and only 4 of those keep
  their sign on the holdout.
- **It does not transfer across guild sets.** Across disjoint guild sets the gear-controlled lifts
  agree at half the rate shared lifts would.
- **Within guild it is at the noise floor.**
  - The true spread of within-guild lifts is about 1.5 win-rate points, and one weapon's estimate has
    a standard error of about 1.5 points.
  - Within-guild lifts correlate r = 0.33 between the training split and the holdout, which share
    guilds, and r = 0.21 across disjoint guild halves. Noise around that spread allows 0.24-0.25.
  - Halberd, Soulscythe and Greataxe are within-guild candidates for a re-test on later folds. They
    are hypotheses, not findings.
- **Pairs and copy counts carry no reliable signal.**
  - The numbers-only pair terms replicate below their own noise ceiling.
  - Under the controls only Energy Shaper + Malevolent Locus survives every level, a candidate.
  - Of the 13 numbers-only copy contrasts, 6 flip on the holdout and none survives item power.
- **Consequence for the prior** (BACKLOG; design §8.6, a prior adjuster, never the primary term):
  - A numbers-controlled per-weapon lift fed to the prior would hand it the choices of well-geared
    guilds, which is popularity among winners again, not effectiveness.
  - A lift that enters the prior needs at least the item-power and guild controls.
  - At 16,638 training parties those controls leave per-weapon lifts unresolved. Resolving them takes
    roughly four times the labelled corpus. The `v4h` A/B gate (standing rule 16) would test a
    controlled lift, not the raw one.
- **Caveats.** All of this is association, not cause.
  - Skill is controlled only through the guild.
  - Item power comes from killer, victim and participant sightings.
  - A killer party that scored no kill is never recorded.
  - The roster names who fought, never who fought whom.
