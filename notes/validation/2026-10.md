## 2026-10-03 — The gear-active doctrine and the gear pools

**Context.** A gear piece's active ability was picked by
`default_gear_choice()`: the sheet option worth most under the template's
weights. Where a strong-on-paper ability is never taken that credits
phantom supply; the Cleric Cowl case (every recording build runs Ice
Block, the sheet scored Force Field higher) was closed by DROPPING the
Force Field rows from the sheet, which made the sheet say what is worn
instead of what the item can do. The tree-shared actives were uncurated:
every cloth head carries Energizing Shield and Force Field beside its own
third active, every plate helmet Energizing Shield and Stone Skin, every
pair of boots Dodge and a sprint; only the items whose own active they
happened to be cited them. Measured: 4 of 129 gear items offered the
engine more than one scored active.

The sources that record which active a player equipped: the Character
Builder comps (`spells_verbatim`, the game's UniqueNames per slot; 33
members across three comps) and the MetaBattle batch (`gear_spells_raw`,
"Active, Passive, ..." display names; 46 builds). The killer-party harvest
records equipment only. On the 79 recording builds every head, armor and
shoes piece is worn for its OWN active; no shared active is chosen once
(the one exception, a single Soldier Boots build on Rejuvenating Sprint,
sits under the floor).

**Decision.**

1. Gear pools (`pipeline/sheets/gear/pools/<tree>.yaml`, nine files: the
   three armor classes by head, armor and shoes). The two actives a tree's
   items share are curated once and composed into every item whose dumps
   menu carries the spell (`sheets_lib.compose_gear`; the item's own row
   wins on a (cap, evidence) tie, `except:` opts out), the weapon pools'
   structure one layer over. Scores (curation judgment, the rubric's
   anchors beside them): Energizing Shield tankiness 1; Force Field
   knockback_displace 3 / peel 3 / anti_dive 2 (the Cleric Cowl's former
   rows); Cleanse cleanse 3 / tankiness 2; Stone Skin tankiness 2; Frost
   Shield tankiness 2 / reflect 1; Inferno Shield tankiness 2 / reflect 2;
   Dodge disengage 2 / mobility 2; the three sprints mobility 2, Refreshing
   Sprint sustained_dps 1, Rejuvenating Sprint self_sustain 2. Taunt and
   Mend Wounds score nothing (no groundable capability; out of combat).
   The lint checks every pool row sits on a menu in its tree and grounds
   its cap. 66 items now offer more than one scored active.
2. The recorded active per worn piece travels through the builds index:
   `build_builds.gear_spell_picks` keeps a pick only as a spell id on the
   WORN item's dumps menu (UniqueNames read directly, MetaBattle names
   through the spell index, a name two spells share resolves to nothing);
   anything else is None.
3. The gear-active doctrine (`build_dataset.resolve_active_doctrine`):
   every head, armor and shoes item is stamped `doctrine_active` — the
   modal recorded active at two votes or more (`observed`), else the
   item's own active, the one no tree-mate shares (`assumed`); the votes
   are tallied by band too (gang <= 9, group 10+, from the recording
   build's party size) for a pick that splits by scale. Quarantined
   records never vote. An observed or assumed active with no scored row
   gets an EMPTY bundle in the loadout's active slot: choosing it supplies
   nothing, never the next-best scored ability.
4. Both engine ports (`default_gear_choice` / `defaultGearChoice`) follow
   the stamp: the band's pick where the band has one, else the overall;
   among the combos carrying that active the argmax settles any other
   slot. The template-weighted argmax across the whole menu remains only
   for an item with no stamp (statless items: capes, offhands, potions,
   food). `gear_choice_source` names the rule per item; `gear_active_spell`
   names the spell. The kit editor and the member popout state each worn
   piece's active with its basis (observed n of m, assumed, unscored).

The fallback is the measured regularity, not a convenience: 79 of 79
recording builds wear a piece for its own active, so an unrecorded item
assumes its own, and the pool's shared shove or sprint scores only where a
build records it. The floor stays at two votes (thin evidence is absent):
Soldier Boots, one vote on Rejuvenating Sprint, assumes Wanderlust and
supplies nothing from the slot until a second build records it.

**Evidence.** 81 head / armor / shoes items stamped: 34 observed, 47
assumed; every observed pick unanimous (votes = of). Cleric Cowl Ice Block
5/5, Assassin Hood Meditation 22/22, Royal Sandals Defenseless Rush
21/21, Hellion Jacket Life Steal Aura 13/13, Knight Helmet Displacement
Immunity 10/10, Judicator Helmet Electric Discharge 3/3. Under
blackzone_roam at 20 the Force Field bundle outscores the Scholar Cowl's
own Aegis of Energy and the engine still assumes the latter (F36d).
Gates after the change: golden 77/77 (T47 holds), forge 88/88 (F36a-h),
evidence-layer 61/61 (H23), parity on 60 random parties plus the per-item
pick at every case's content and size, layout, codec, roles, skeletons,
validation modes.

(`load_gear_pools`, `gear_tree`, `compose_gear`), `pipeline/build_dataset.py`

## 2026-10-03 — The account layer: a pinned library, one type scale, the history's dates

**Context.** Three open items on the account layer's page chrome. The
page loaded `@supabase/supabase-js@2`, a floating major version, with no
integrity check: the one network dependency of the page could change
under it. `_auth.css` set ten literal font sizes between 10 and 23
pixels. The history tables carried the full local time with its UTC in a
column, the widest cell of each row.

**Decision.**

1. The library is pinned to the version the floating tag served on the
   day (2.117.2, `dist/umd/supabase.js`) with a sha384
   subresource-integrity hash and anonymous CORS; the browser refuses a
   bundle whose bytes differ. Moving the version changes the URL and the
   hash together (`dashboard/build.py`).
2. The account layer's type is one six-step scale declared once
   (`--fs-label` 10, `--fs-meta` 11, `--fs-dense` 12, `--fs-body` 13,
   `--fs-lead` 16, `--fs-title` 21); every font-size in `_auth.css` is a
   step. 14 folds into body, 15 and 18 into lead, 23 into title; the
   label and meta steps and the dense and body steps stay apart (mono
   capitals against buttons, table controls against running text). On a
   phone a field keeps the lead step.
3. A history table's date cell shows the day alone in the reader's zone
   (the year once it is not the current one); the full local time with
   its UTC is the cell's title.

**Evidence.** The pinned file fetched twice hashes identically (217,945
bytes). 82 font-size declarations rewritten, none literal after. Pins:
L27s, L39o, three date-label checks in `test_history.js`.

**Changes.** `dashboard/build.py`, `dashboard/_auth.css`,
`dashboard/_history.js`, `dashboard/_portal.html` (the planner's chip and
radius set), `tests/test_dashboard_layout.py` (L27s, L39o),
`tests/test_history.js`, `BACKLOG.md`, `tests/VALIDATION.md`.

## 2026-10-04 — One killboard sampler

**Context.** Three samplers read the killboard. `sample_battles.py`
(albionbb kill events, killer and victim) wrote `weapon_usage_v2.json`:
fight-size prevalence and organization cohorts, players grouped by the
alliance or guild the feed states. `sample_rosters.py` wrote
`roster_mixes.json`: wiped sides at alliance level. `sample_parties.py`
(the official API's `GroupMembers`) harvests the killer's party with gear.
Measured on the committed artifacts: the usage sample held 900 battles
(577 / 283 / 40 at small / mid / large) and 7,811 player-weapon pairs and
was last written on 2026-08-29; the rosters artifact holds 41,853 battles
of 6+ players in its newest 28 days with 586,447 builds, and 36,367 /
28,848 / 10,521 killer parties of 2-5 / 6-15 / 16+ with two distinct
catalogue weapons. `roster_mixes.json` has no code reader and its cache
stopped growing on 2026-08-26. The party harvest records killer parties
only (a party that scored no kill is never recorded), so it cannot
re-derive a wiped side; its builds carry no alliance.

**Decision.** (maintainer decision on the cohort unit, the frame, the
frozen record and the cleanup; the thresholds are curation judgment)

1. The party harvest is the one killboard sampler. `derive_usage.py`
   derives `weapon_usage_v2.json` from `party_rosters.json.gz`, offline
   and byte-identically; `sample_battles.py`, `sample_rosters.py` and
   `daily_fetch.ps1` are removed and the fold has no network step.
2. The frame: every harvested battle (battle list and kill-feed poll) of
   6+ players that started in the 28 days before the newest battle in the
   artifact. The window is anchored on the data, never on the clock.
3. Prevalence stays on the FIGHT-size axis, over combatants with a build:
   killers, victims and kill participants, one build per player per
   battle. A loadout swap inside a battle is not observable and is stored
   as unknown.
4. A cohort is one KILLER PARTY, on the PARTY-size axis: a party of N keys
   to the bucket a fight of 2N falls in (2-5 / 6-15 / 16+), the mapping
   `size_bucket` and `usageBucket` already apply. The party the killer
   belonged to is the evidence unit (the kit doctrine's rule); a five-man
   squad inside a large fight is not evidence for a 20-man plan.
5. Each bucket keeps 1,000 parties spread evenly over the window in
   (start, battle, party index) order, never the newest 1,000 (at 16+
   that is three days of fights). `cohort_meta` states the parties in the
   window beside the parties kept. The page embeds baskets as indexes
   into a key table.
6. The observed families keep the anchor pair and gain two gate changes.
   The cohort floor is max(5, 2% of the bucket's usable cohorts): at
   1,000 cohorts a bucket the absolute floor of 5 admitted 44 / 40 / 15
   families. A distinct organization is a group of parties linked by a
   shared guild, transitively: a guild fielding its lineup with different
   guests is one organization; an unguilded party adds none.
7. `roster_mixes.json` is frozen: the record stays as the evidence the
   need profiles in `roles.yaml` cite, marked `frozen` in its `_meta`.
8. The copy says "killer parties" and "fielded together", never "winning
   comp": a killer party scored at least one kill, so the sample leans to
   the winning side.

**Evidence.** On the committed rosters artifact (window 2026-09-01 to
2026-09-29): 24,193 / 11,572 / 6,088 battles and 169,128 / 158,056 /
259,263 combatants at small / mid / large. The Hallowfall share of
large-fight combatants reads 11.9% where the kill-event sample read 5.2%:
a healer rarely kills or dies and enters as a kill participant. Median
distinct weapons per cohort 3 / 7 / 15. Families: 4 / 10 / 8 (21 / 20 / 5
on the earlier sample under the absolute floor); the 16+ lead is
Realmbreaker with Spirithunter (428 of 1,000 cohorts, 48 organizations,

untested (BACKLOG). The planner page is 5,390 KB (5,534 KB before; the
per-battle index is no longer embedded). Display only: no engine input
changes, the dataset is untouched.

## 2026-10-04 — Form abilities score on the E; anti-dive counts ally protection

**Context.** A Dragon Portal seven (Polehammer, Rootbound Staff, Enigmatic
Staff, Realmbreaker, Dawnsong, Mistpiercer, Redemption Staff) read on the
board: no anti-dive from its two supports, one unit of sustained healing
from the Rootbound, burst healing under the pool's typical. Checked
against the sheets and the dumps at the pinned snapshot:

1. The Rootbound sheet scored the E's own text alone (Growth: +8% Max and
   Current Health in 11m): buff_allies 4, heal_sustain 2. A shapeshifter
   form's two abilities and its passive carry their own names and
   descriptions in `spells.json` and sit on no equip menu, so
   `curate_helper.py` never printed them. The Sylvian form's: Seedling's
   Bloom (a ground heal zone cast at 12m on a 3s cooldown; allies inside
   heal every second for 8s, 7 a tick, 14 a tick and +10% Max and Current
   Health once a Shift Charge grows it) and Barbed Roots (a 3s
   uninterruptible, damage-immune channel on a 30s cooldown for three
   Shift Charges: allies within 11m gain a 175 absorb shield and Forced
   Movement immunity for 3s, enemies within 11m are slowed by 50%). The
   same holds for the other seven forms: 25 form abilities in all, 11
   carrying a prose flag no row on the E covers.
2. The Enigmatic sheet scored Protective Beam (a 240 absorb bubble in
   6.5m around the targeted ally, re-applied every second, cast from 14m,
   30s cooldown) as buff_allies 4 and nothing else. anti_dive counted
   effects landed on the diver alone (the effect map's enemy rows); the
   peel rule (2026-08-28) files ally protection under buff_allies and
   tankiness. A bubble on the dived ally is what denies a small-scale
   dive its kill, and no row said so.
3. Burst healing read 2.90 against a typical of 3.14: the Redemption's
   Holy Flash through a Cleric Robe (2.0 x 1.45); its sphere is sustained
   healing, which read 5.35 against 5.08. The row measures what it names;
   nothing to change.

**Decision.** (maintainer decision on both re-scores; every score is
curation judgment)

1. A transform's form abilities are scored on the E: the row cites the
   `SHAPESHIFT_*` spell, and an `add:` entry in `effect_overrides.yaml`
   quotes the form ability's text for each capability the lint checks.
   The text to read is the one the spell's own `@descriptionlocatag`
   names. Barbed Roots points at its `_V2` description; the retired text
   under the id's default tag still states a group cleanse the ability
   no longer has, so the Rootbound carries no cleanse row.
2. Rootbound Staff: heal_sustain 2 to 4 (Seedling's Bloom), peel 2 (the
   Forced Movement immunity), slow 2 (the 50% slow in 11m for the
   channel), anti_dive 2 (the shield on the dived allies and the slow on
   the divers among them). buff_allies stays 4. peel and slow leave the
   tree pool's W rows on this weapon (`except:`): one slot per
   capability, as on every sheet whose E carries a pool capability.
   The E's heal points stay under `e_heal_min`: not a full healer. Its
   heal scale is corrected to group (`heal_overrides.yaml`: the Bloom
   heals 5 allies in a 3 to 6 radius, Growth reaches 11m; the transform's
   own cast facts carry no area and read single).
3. The anti-dive rule: anti_dive is what denies a dive. It counts an
   effect landed on the diver, and a protection the caster places on
   ANOTHER ally at the moment of the dive: an absorb shield, a damage
   immunity or a damage redirection. Neither is peel (peel touches the
