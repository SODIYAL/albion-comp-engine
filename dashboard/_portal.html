    How these portal stats are counted
    • Who counts. A killer party at kill time, one vote per party per battle. Solo is the lone killer. A battle is tagged as a portal fight when any victim in it carried an Ancient Bone or a Drake shard, items found only inside the Ancient Lands, and then every kill in it counts; a portal fight in which no victim carried one is not counted: the numbers undercount, they never invent.
    • Win rate (weapons) counts both sides of every portal kill. Each weapon in the killing party takes an equal share of the kill, so a party's kills count for its healer too, and the victim's weapon takes the death; a death is filed in the pool of the party that made the kill. Across a pool the kills and the deaths balance, so 50% is even: above it a weapon kills more than it dies. Fights are the kills a weapon took part in plus its deaths; the win rate shows from 100 fights. It still carries item power and the players' skill: evidence, not a strength rating.
    • Dominant (comps and shapes) means the party took no deaths in that battle, shown against the pool's own share: every party here scored a kill, so most are dominant and the difference is the read. The victim's party is not in the kill data, so a comp has no two-sided rate. K/D is the party's kills over its deaths, summed over its members. Prevalence is how often a weapon or comp appears among winners; it is not a measure of strength on its own.
    • Builds are the modal item per slot over winning players of that weapon in that pool, one player one vote, shown once three players have worn the slot. Victims' gear is what died and is not shown.
    • Shapes (pools of six and more) count a party's members per role: frontline, healer, support and damage, each weapon read through the role the planner seats it in. An exact comp rarely repeats past five members, so the shape and the role-by-role roster are the reads that recur; the ranges are the middle half of the winning parties.
    • Pools follow the game's matchmaking: solo, 2 to 3, 4 to 5, 6 to 7 and 15 to 20. Small samples are marked; a pool with under 40 parties is a glimpse, not a meta.
