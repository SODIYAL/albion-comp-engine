    How these portal stats are counted
    • Who counts. A killer party at kill time, one vote per party per battle. Solo is the lone killer. The kill is tagged as a portal kill when the victim carried an Ancient Bone, an item that only exists inside the Ancient Lands, so a kill whose victim had not yet earned one is not counted: the numbers undercount, they never invent.
    • Dominant means the party took no deaths in that battle. K/D is the party's kills over its deaths, summed over its members. Prevalence is how often a weapon or comp appears among winners; it is not a measure of strength on its own.
    • Builds are the modal item per slot over winning players of that weapon in that pool, one player one vote, shown once three players have worn the slot. Victims' gear is what died and is not shown.
    • Shapes (pools of six and more) count a party's members per role: frontline, healer, support and damage, each weapon read through the role the planner seats it in. An exact comp rarely repeats past five members, so the shape and the role-by-role roster are the reads that recur; the ranges are the middle half of the winning parties.
    • Pools follow the game's matchmaking: solo, 2 to 3, 4 to 5, 6 to 7 and 15 to 20. Small samples are marked; a pool with under 40 parties is a glimpse, not a meta.
    • This page is a killboard surface. The planner's recommendations do not read it.
